Summer Spa Packages

Summer Spa Packages at Azure Bay

This summer, escape the heat and slow everything down with our ocean-inspired spa experiences, created exclusively for guests of Azure Bay Hotel.

Designed to reflect the calm and rhythm of the coastline, our summer spa packages combine restorative treatments, natural marine elements, and private wellness facilities for a truly immersive retreat.

Signature Summer Treatments

Full-Body Massage Therapy

Ease tension and restore balance with tailored massage treatments using aromatic oils inspired by the sea.

Sea-Salt Body Scrubs

Naturally exfoliating scrubs made with mineral-rich sea salts to refresh the skin and stimulate circulation.

Private Wellness Access

All summer spa packages include access to our private wellness facilities, offering guests a quiet space to unwind before or after treatments.

  • Thermal relaxation rooms
  • Sauna and steam facilities
  • Ocean-view relaxation areas

Seasonal Offers

Our summer spa packages are available at special seasonal rates and can be added to any stay. Whether you’re visiting for a weekend or an extended escape, these treatments are designed to complement your time at Azure Bay.

Availability is limited during peak periods. We recommend booking spa treatments in advance to secure your preferred time.

Live Music Every Friday

Live Music Every Friday at Azure Bay

As the sun sets over the bay, Azure Bay Hotel comes alive with live music, handcrafted cocktails, and a relaxed coastal atmosphere every Friday evening.

Our weekly live music sessions are designed for guests who want to unwind, socialise, and enjoy quality performances without leaving the comfort of the hotel.

An Evening by the Bay

Performances take place in our lounge area, offering panoramic views of Azure Bay and comfortable seating throughout the space. Music styles vary from acoustic sets and jazz-inspired sessions to relaxed contemporary covers.

Local Artists & Signature Cocktails

Live Performances

We collaborate with talented local musicians to bring a fresh and authentic sound to each Friday night.

Cocktails & Refreshments

Enjoy a curated cocktail menu, premium wines, and light bites available throughout the evening.

Event Details

  • Every Friday evening
  • Live performances from early evening
  • Open to hotel guests
  • No reservation required

Seating is available on a first-come basis. Guests are encouraged to arrive early to secure their preferred spot and enjoy the full experience.

New Rooftop Pool

The New Rooftop Pool at Azure Bay

We are delighted to welcome guests to our newly renovated rooftop pool, offering uninterrupted views across Azure Bay and a refined space designed for relaxation.

Positioned above the coastline, the rooftop pool combines modern design, comfortable lounging areas, and attentive service, creating an elevated experience for guests staying at Azure Bay Hotel.

A Space Designed to Unwind

The redesigned rooftop area has been thoughtfully created to balance style and comfort. Guests can enjoy spacious seating, shaded areas, and a calm atmosphere throughout the day.

Panoramic Views & Poolside Service

Uninterrupted Coastal Views

Take in sweeping views of the bay while enjoying the serenity of the rooftop setting, from early morning through sunset.

Dedicated Poolside Service

Guests can order refreshments and light bites directly to their loungers, allowing for a seamless and relaxed experience.

Guest Access & Information

  • Exclusively available to hotel guests
  • Open daily during the summer season
  • Loungers available on a first-come basis
  • Relaxed, adults-friendly atmosphere

We recommend visiting during quieter morning hours or early evening for the most relaxed experience. Pool access is included with your stay at Azure Bay.

Sustainability at Azure Bay

Sustainability at Azure Bay

At Azure Bay Hotel, we are dedicated to responsible hospitality that protects our beautiful coastline and supports the local community.

Our sustainability initiatives focus on reducing environmental impact, supporting regional suppliers, and creating a greener, cleaner experience for our guests.

Eco-Friendly Practices

We implement energy-efficient lighting, water-saving technologies, and waste reduction programs throughout the hotel. From housekeeping to kitchen operations, sustainability is embedded in our daily routine.

Supporting Local Suppliers

Farm-to-Table Dining

Our kitchens source fresh ingredients from local farms and suppliers, reducing carbon footprint and supporting regional businesses.

Community Engagement

We partner with local artisans, craftspeople, and service providers to enhance guest experiences while fostering sustainable local economies.

Protecting Our Coastline

Azure Bay Hotel actively participates in beach cleanups, marine conservation initiatives, and educational programs to protect the natural environment that surrounds us.

Guest Involvement

  • Informational guides about sustainability efforts
  • Optional participation in eco-activities
  • Recycling and energy-saving amenities in rooms

Guests staying at Azure Bay are encouraged to support our sustainable initiatives, helping to create a positive impact on the local environment while enjoying their stay.
